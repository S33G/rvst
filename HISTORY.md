# Development history

RVST was implemented and integrated on 30 October 2026. At the project owner's
request, the initial source import is split into small topical commits whose
author and committer dates span October 2026. Those dates are reconstructed;
they are not a record of a month of elapsed implementation or testing.

Validation reports record the actual verification date. Subsequent work uses
its actual date. Every commit carries `Co-Author: Charlie's Agent`.
